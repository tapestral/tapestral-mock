Our Mission

tapestral gives interior designers direct access to the vendors they already work with. We handle sourcing, quoting, and order workflows so designers can stay focused on their clients.

On the vendor side, we remove operational overhead so manufacturers can focus on building great products. The result is a more connected and efficient trade workflow.
