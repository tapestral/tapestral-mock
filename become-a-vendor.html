The platform

A Fully Built Solution

tapestral is not a listing site nor a third-party marketplace. It is a complete operating layer between your products and the designers who specify them. tapestral handles every step from catalog presentation through order confirmation, payment, and fulfillment, automatically.

01

Automated Orders

When a designer places an order, tapestral sends you a structured order with everything you need.

02

Data-Driven Catalog

tapestral syncs your product data so your catalog stays current with pricing, specs, and availability for designers.

03

Product Customization

Finish options, fabrics, and dimensions are structured directly into the ordering flow for clear selection at checkout.

04

Fast, Secure Payments

Payment processing runs directly to your account with no credit card surcharges on ACH.

End to end

What You Get

01

Your catalog, beautifully presented

Your products are displayed with high-resolution imagery, detailed specs, finish options, and customization choices in a professional interface built for the design trade.

02

Automated order notification

Every order generates a structured notification with line items, customization selections, shipping method, and designer contact information. Orders and status updates move automatically between designers and vendors throughout fulfillment.

03

Data-driven catalog

Your product data can be updated in real time or through your existing workflow, ensuring designers always source from current pricing, specs, and availability.

04

Configurable customization engine

Define finishes, fabrics, COM allowances, and size variants. Designers select from structured options that are captured cleanly in each order, reducing ambiguity in production.

05

Built-in support

Our team is available to help resolve order issues, clarify designer questions, and ensure a smooth experience on both sides of every transaction.

06

A new sales channel, zero effort

Once live, your catalog is continuously accessible to vetted interior designers sourcing products for active projects.
