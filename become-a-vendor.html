The platform

A Fully Built Solution

tapestral is not a listing site nor a 3rd-party marketplace. It is a complete operating layer between your products and the designers who specify them. tapestral handles every step from catalog presentation through order confirmation, payment, and fulfillment data, automatically.

01

Automated Orders

When a designer places an order, tapestral automatically sends you a structured order with everything you need. line items, customization selections, shipping method, and contact info. No manual hand-off from our side.

02

Data-Driven Catalog

tapestral updates product data as often as you update it, ensuring your catalog is always current and accurate.

03

Product Customization

Support finish selections, fabric options, COM requests, and dimension variations, all presented cleanly to designers at the point of ordering. Customization logic is built into the platform, not bolted on.

04

Fast, Secure Payments

Payment processing runs directly to your account with no credit card surcharges on ACH.

End to end

What You Get

01

Your catalog, beautifully presented

Your products are displayed with high-resolution imagery, detailed specs, finish options, and customization choices in a professional interface built for the design trade. tapestral handles the presentation layer and you supply the data.

02

Automated order notification

Every order triggers an automatic notification to you with the full order details. Line items, customization selections, shipping method, and designer contact. tapestral handles the send; you handle the fulfillment. Status updates and acknowledgements are automatically communicated back to the designer as the order progresses.

03

Data-driven catalog

tapestral updates product data as often as you update it, ensuring your catalog is always current and accurate.

04

Configurable customization engine

Define the options you offer. finish codes, fabric grades, COM allowances, size variants and tapestral surfaces them to designers as structured choices at checkout. Every selection is captured cleanly in the order record, eliminating the ambiguity that causes production errors.

05

Built-in support

Our team is available to help resolve order issues, clarify designer questions, and ensure a smooth experience on both sides of every transaction. The platform handles the routine; we handle the exceptions.
