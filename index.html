Shipping Built for How You Work

Not every order ships the same way. Choose LTL freight for cost-effective pallet delivery, or white glove for premium inside placement — both backed by Averitt.

01

LTL Freight

Cost-effective shared-truck delivery for larger shipments. Best for pieces that ship on a pallet to a dock or residential address. Live quotes, instant dispatch, auto-generated BOLs.

02

White Glove

Premium inside delivery with careful placement by trained crews. The right choice when your client's pieces require more than a curbside drop. Full tracking, full care.

03

Bring Your Own Carrier

Prefer your existing freight relationship? Select your carrier at checkout. You arrange pickup — we get out of the way. Full flexibility, your terms.

04

Real-Time Tracking

Know where your clients' pieces are at all times. Automatic status updates, freight audit flags, and complete documentation in your account.
